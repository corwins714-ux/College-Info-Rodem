How to Fast Track College Degrees

- Steps to complete degrees quickly are listed below, with the priority being time savings rather than cutting costs - though these steps accomplish both

- None of the steps are mandatory, and the sequence is flexible, since each person’s preference, capabilities, and specific degree requirements may be different

- The American Council on Education (ACE) & National College Credit Recommendation Service (NCCRS) are national organizations that evaluate online courses and training programs, certify that they are college‑level and recommend how many credits universities can accept


Fast‑Track College Plan (Simple Version)

Step 1 - Knock out free credits first

  • Take Modern States courses
  • Get free CLEP vouchers
  • Pass CLEP exams (aim for 6–10 exams)

Step 2 - Add DSST exams if needed

  • Use DSST for subjects CLEP doesn’t cover
  • Target 6–12 credits

Step 3 - Fill remaining credits with Sophia.org

  • $99/month unlimited courses
  • No proctored exams
  • Finish 6–12 courses per month

Step 4 - Choose your course format (real-world options)

  • Competency-Based Education (CBE)
    • WGU - CBE with subscription-style 6-month terms
    • UMPI YourPace - project-based, no proctored exams

  • Accelerated Online Courses
    • 6-week courses
    • 8-week courses
    • 12-week courses
    • Common at community colleges

  • Traditional Semester Courses
    • Standard university classes
    • Slowest pace, highest cost

  • Hybrid / Evening In-Person Courses
    • Example: UMGC Pearl City
    • 6pm–9pm classes
    • Instructor physically present

  • Fully In-Person Courses
    • On-campus lectures
    • Labs, group work, attendance requirements

  • Self-Paced Online Courses
    • Sophia.org
    • Study.com
    • StraighterLine

Step 5 - Finish remaining courses at your chosen school

  • Excelsior: 7–12 credits in‑house
  • UMPI: 30 credits in‑house
  • WGU: finish as fast as possible
