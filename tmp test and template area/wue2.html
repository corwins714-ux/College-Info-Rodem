Degrees Not Offered in Hawaiʻi and How WUE Reduces Their Costs

In some cases, WUE gives students access to entire majors we don’t even have in Hawaiʻi. These degrees normally require paying full out‑of‑state tuition, but WUE can cut those costs by more than 50%.

For example, majors like Forensic Anthropology and Aerospace Engineering drop from full out‑of‑state pricing to less than half under WUE — instantly turning impossible degrees into realistic options for Hawaiʻi students.

Here are examples of degrees unavailable in Hawaiʻi and their respective cost advantages through WUE:

    Forensic Anthropology — Hawaiʻi has anthropology but no forensic anthro track.

    Aerospace Engineering — UH Mānoa has engineering but no aerospace degree.

    Game Design / Simulation / Esports Management — UH has digital media but no full game design major.

    Nuclear Engineering — UH has engineering but no nuclear program.

    Veterinary Technology / Pre Vet — Hawaiʻi has zero vet tech programs.

    Cognitive Science — UH has psychology but no cognitive science major.

    Outdoor Recreation Leadership / Wilderness EMT — UH has kinesiology but no wilderness leadership programs.

    Historic Preservation / Archaeological Field Tech — UH has archaeology but no historic‑preservation degree.

    Fire Science / Wildland Fire Management — Hawaiʻi has EMT programs but no fire‑science degrees.
