NSA - Stokes Educational Scholarship Program - an NSA pre‑employment pipeline

     • NSA pays your full tuition for your last 2 years of college

     • You get a salary while in school and work full‑time two summers at NSA while in college (40 hours per week - Monday–Friday)

     • You get a guaranteed job at NSA after graduation

     • You owe them 1.5 years of work for each year they paid (2 years of college paid ×1.5 years owed = 3 years, but there is more to this)

     • The two summers you work at NSA while in college reduces your post‑graduation obligation, you typically end up owing closer to 2.5 years after graduation

     • You basically become a civilian NSA employee-in-training your last two years of collge


NSA Stokes - Allowed Degree Programs

     • Computer Science - core software, algorithms, secure systems

     • Computer Engineering - hardware, embedded systems, digital logic

     • Electrical Engineering - circuits, signals, comms, radio frequencies

     • Chinese Language - Mandarin-focused language analysis track


Benefits for the Student

     • NSA pays for your last two years of college

     • Students are paid as a federal civilian employees (with Hawai‘i locality) year round for the last two years of college - either GS‑3 ≈ $40,000–$42,000 per year, or GS‑4 ≈ $45K–$48K

     • Full‑time paid summers - 40‑hour weeks inside NSA, real work, real moneyp

     • Guaranteed job - full‑time NSA position waiting for you after graduation

     • Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Intelligenge (TS/SCI) security clearance - NSA sponsors your clearance, worth tens of thousands

     • Federal benefits - health, retirement pipeline, locality pay, leave accrual

     • Career pipeline - direct path into cyber, intel, engineering, math, language roles

     • Reduced service obligation - summer work counts toward your required service

     • Professional experience - real classified mission work before you even graduate

     • Financial stability - salary + tuition = no debt and guaranteed employment

     • International employment opportunities - foreign assignments possible in ~10–15 countries, NSA does not publicly disclose specific nations for security

     • Language training - access to advanced foreign‑language schools and immersion programs
